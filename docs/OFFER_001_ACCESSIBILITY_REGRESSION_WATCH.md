# Offer 001 - Accessibility Regression Watch

## Status

**EXPERIMENT - manual commercial proof required**

This offer is deliberately bounded before any real outreach or customer delivery is automated.

## Buyer hypothesis

Primary ICP:

- web/digital agencies managing multiple client websites
- maintenance retainers or recurring web support already exist
- buyer can benefit from a portfolio-wide regression signal without adding another manual QA ritual

Secondary ICPs to test later:

- vendors maintaining state/local government websites
- multi-site organizations with centralized web teams
- ecommerce operators with frequent frontend changes

## Outcome

Continuously detect machine-testable accessibility regressions on agreed public pages, preserve evidence, prioritize changes, and produce a client-ready regression report.

DexFoundry must **not** sell or claim:

- guaranteed ADA compliance
- guaranteed WCAG conformance
- legal certification
- complete accessibility coverage from automation alone

Automated checks are an evidence source and regression detector. Human accessibility review remains necessary for issues automation cannot establish.

## Passive pre-sales proof

For a manually selected prospect:

1. Choose at most three public pages.
2. Run a bounded automated accessibility scan.
3. Normalize findings into DexFoundry evidence records.
4. Identify only directly observed machine-testable issues.
5. Build a short POC snapshot with evidence references.
6. Human reviews the POC before any use in outreach.

No authentication bypass, crawling behind login, intrusive testing, automated form submission, or destructive interaction is authorized.

## Trigger signals

Possible qualification signals include:

- agency manages multiple client sites
- accessibility/QA/web-maintenance hiring
- recent redesign or CMS migration
- public-sector web work
- sampled public pages contain machine-detectable error-level findings

These signals are inputs to the existing deterministic DexFoundry score. They are not proof of willingness to buy.

## Delivery hypothesis

If manually proven, recurring delivery could include:

- scheduled scans of agreed pages
- new/resolved/regressed finding deltas
- evidence retention
- severity/rule grouping
- regression alerts
- monthly client-ready reports
- human-review queue for ambiguous/manual-only checks

## Manual proof required before automation expansion

Minimum evidence before real outreach automation or customer delivery automation:

- at least 5 manually selected prospects scanned
- at least 3 POCs judged accurate and useful by a human reviewer
- at least 2 real buyer conversations about the offer
- at least 1 buyer demonstrates willingness to pay or signs a pilot
- scan runtime/cost and false-positive burden are recorded
- a repeat scan demonstrates useful change/regression detection

Until then, the offer remains an experiment and outbound remains human-gated.

## Success metrics to record

- pages scanned
- scan duration
- machine-testable errors found
- false-positive/irrelevant finding count after human review
- POC preparation minutes
- buyer response
- pilot conversion
- recurring scan cost
- number of new/resolved/regressed findings on repeat scan

## Source/market rationale

Current external evidence supports the mechanism rather than a guarantee of market fit:

- WCAG evaluation cannot be reduced to automated testing alone; knowledgeable human evaluation remains necessary.
- DOJ Title II web/mobile accessibility requirements create a concrete accessibility deadline for U.S. state and local governments, using WCAG 2.1 Level AA.
- Mature commercial products already sell recurring accessibility monitoring, which validates the monitoring category while also proving DexFoundry must differentiate through service workflow, evidence, and agency-friendly reporting rather than "we run a scanner."

Market fit, pricing, and willingness-to-pay remain unproven until manual prospect work is completed.
